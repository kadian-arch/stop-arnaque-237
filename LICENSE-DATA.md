> Stop Arnaque 237: Cameroon Scam Reports Dataset. https://github.com/kadian-arch/stop-arnaque-237

Public alert summaries are written by the project. Article-based alerts link to their original source (named in its `source` field), whose articles remain the property of their publishers. Alerts from public social media posts keep only the scam message and a summary written by the project, with no link to the post. Short scam messages quoted by a source (`example_message`) are reproduced as evidence of the scam, anonymized.
